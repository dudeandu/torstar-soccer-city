The fan: Financial analyst Jake Kim, 37

Cheering for: South Korea, a country that’s made eleven World Cup appearances. South Korea has a population of 51.75 million people and is a country in East Asia located in the southern half of the Korean Peninsula;

Why he's cheering: Jake Kim remembers the time his home country hosted the World Cup.

Born and raised in South Korea, Kim spent his youth falling in love with the game. He grew up playing soccer but knew he would be a fan for life when South Korea hosted the 2002 World Cup.

“I truly became a fan watching all those matches happening in my homeland,” said Kim. “That's what I fell in love with.”

That love of the sport carried on even when Kim left South Korea. When he came to Toronto he joined the Korean Canadian Soccer Association. He started playing in an amateur soccer league at Esther Shiner Civic Stadium in North York.

He realized the fondest memories he made was not watching soccer but taking to the field.

“I think the game that I typically play myself has much of a bigger impact,” said Kim. “Whether you win or lose it's bound that you can have a good time and that's where I really got pulled into playing soccer.”

Kim played on that field for 10 years, winning tournaments, going neck-and-neck with other teams to the bitter end, and enjoying the time he spent with lifelong friends. The league even allows people of different skills and ages to play together.

Now as the World Cup heads to his new home, Kim is ready to soak it all in and enjoy getting together with his league as they cheer on South Korea.

“Hosting the World Cup, it only comes once in a lifetime,” said Kim.”I'm just excited to really see that happening.”

How to cheer:

“It's just a bunch of our soccer buddies that we are expected to gather at the pub to watch the game together,” said Kim.
